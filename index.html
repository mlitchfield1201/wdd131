Canada’s Flag

The national flag of Canada was adopted in 1965. It was designed by George Stanley, a historian at Mount Allison University. Before this flag, the country used a version of the Union Jack, symbolizing it as a commonwealth nation. The maple leaf has been a symbol of Canada since the 19th century. The red and white used on the flag are now the official colors of Canada.
